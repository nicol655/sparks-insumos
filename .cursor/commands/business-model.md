# Capture business-model context

Add or update everything about the business model in the shared context, so every
agent and contributor builds features aligned with how the business actually works.

Do this:

1. Read the current `.ai/business-model.md`, `.ai/project.md`, and `.ai/glossary.md`.
2. Gather the business context from what the user provides after the command
   and from any docs/links they point to. If key pieces are missing (value
   proposition, customer segments, revenue model, North Star metric, hard
   constraints), **ask focused questions** — one short batch, don't guess.
3. Fill `.ai/business-model.md`, replacing `_TBD_`: value proposition, customer
   segments, problem/JTBD, solution & key features, revenue model & pricing, key
   metrics, market & competition, constraints & compliance, go-to-market, risks.
4. Add any new domain terms to `.ai/glossary.md`.
5. If a business fact implies a hard product constraint (e.g. compliance,
   performance budget, pricing gate), also add it to `.ai/constitution.md`.
6. Update `.ai/progress.md` and summarize what changed.

Keep it concrete and current — delete stale statements instead of adding caveats.
This is context, not a pitch: prefer facts and numbers over adjectives.
